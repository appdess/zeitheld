## Bundled fictional heroes

The `HeroNova`, `HeroGearwing`, `HeroCoral`, and `HeroEmber` images are original,
AI-assisted fictional character concepts created for ZeitHeld during private
prototyping in July 2026. They were intentionally designed without franchise
names, commercial superhero logos, copied costumes, weapons, or celebrity
likenesses.

## Generated user backgrounds

The Hero Lab requests a completely original fictional character, blocks common
franchise/brand references, prohibits logos and copied characters, and
moderates both the prompt and returned image. Those safeguards reduce risk but
cannot guarantee that a model output is unique or non-infringing. Generated
backgrounds remain local to the device and are not uploaded to this repository.
